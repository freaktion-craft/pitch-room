One-time setup

Stem separation uses Demucs (PyTorch), beat tracking uses librosa, YouTube links use yt-dlp, and the note bars in Practice come from the lead vocal alone, which a karaoke model (via audio-separator) splits from backing vocals and vocal chops. Lyrics come from LRCLIB and are lined up with the voice by Whisper. Together they’re a 3–4 GB download, installed into this app’s own folder.
